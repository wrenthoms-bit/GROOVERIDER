Connect MIDI, then play: notes become the cloud’s pitch centres (middle C = the source’s own pitch) and chords spread the grains across the held notes. Scale-lock snaps them into the key.
Grains come from the shared C++ core (WebAssembly) — the same engine as the Android app. Chaos, scale-lock, reverb and shimmer are a web layer around it. Key is relative to the source’s own pitch (the demo source is in C).

How to play

If you hear nothing

  • Tap Enter the Observatory (or Play) first. Browsers won’t make sound until you tap something on the page.
  • iPhone and iPad: turn the silent switch off. Browser audio stays muted while the phone is on silent, even with the volume up.
  • Headphones help. The stereo width and the drones are a big part of the sound, and phone speakers lose most of that.
  • MIDI controllers only work in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Everything else works on phones.

Choose a sound

  • Samples… loads a built-in sound. Import audio (or drag a file onto the page) uses your own, up to 60 seconds. Record mic captures up to 10 seconds.
  • The menu under the title switches presets. Re-roll gives a new variation with the same settings.
  • Long, sustained sounds — voice, strings, field recordings — make the best pads and drones.
  • Your audio never leaves this device; everything runs in the browser.

Shape it

  • Performance field: drag left and right to move through the sound, up and down to make it brighter or darker.
  • TEXTURE goes from long, smooth grains to short, dense ones.
  • DRIFT sets how far and how fast the sound wanders on its own. Low drifts over minutes; high churns.
  • SPACE is the reverb, from a room to an ocean. PITCH is how far the grains scatter in pitch.
  • DRONE holds one spot in the sound and lets it evolve endlessly.
  • KEY and SCALE keep everything in tune. Choose Free to unlock. Key assumes the sound is in C.
  • Detail opens the fine controls: shimmer, tone, register and more.

Controls

  • Rings: drag up or down, scroll, or use the arrow keys. Hold Shift for fine steps. Double-click returns to the preset value.
  • Space plays and stops. Esc closes panels.
  • Render WAV saves 8 seconds plus the reverb tail to your downloads.

MIDI keyboard and knobs

  • Click MIDI and allow access. Then just play: notes set the pitch, and chords spread the cloud across the notes.
  • In Detail, Latch keeps a chord after you let go; Gate only sounds while keys are held.
  • Knobs: click Learn, click a control’s MIDI badge, then turn a knob. For Play, Re-roll or Drone, click the button, then hit a pad.
  • Your mappings are saved in this browser.

Tips

  • For a drone: DRONE on, SPACE high, DRIFT low. Then leave it alone for a minute — it keeps changing.
  • For movement: DRONE off, raise DRIFT, and nudge Scan in Detail.
  • Shimmer adds a halo an octave up. A little goes a long way.
  • If the sound crackles, lower Density in Detail or close other tabs.
